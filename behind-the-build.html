Behind the build

The idea

I had to collect furniture from a neighbour of my parents over the weekend, the sellers were getting rid of their aunts furniture and household items as she had moved into a home.

This got me thinking about possible solutions for people who are clearing out relatives houses, instead of sending a generic WhatsApp text around into groups stating “clearing out house we have x,y & z to go” a one stop solution to post your items, arrange collection and reduce separate whatsapp back and forth could be a good example for this CA.

Behind the build

Area 1: The Problem and who has it?

This idea is targeted at relatives of home owner’s who have passed away or moved into cared homing. The individuals who have to help clear a relatives home or remove, sell or offload the relative’s furniture and household items.

These relatives can opt for limited free listings of items for collection or opt to pay a small premium for significantly more or unlimited listings of items.

In their words

“We had to get rid of my Aunt Cathy’s items after she moved into a care home, we didn’t have the money for removal service and listing all her items individually on various sites was taking too much time.” (fictious quote) Irene O’Connor
“The majority of selling websites limit you to one item per posting, this can be a serious pain when you are already overwhelmed with just how many items one person can accumulate over the years.” James McGettigan (fictious quote)

Area 2

The solution fits the requirements.

A one stop site that allows users to upload items directly from their phones, allowing bulk uploads meaning the user can walk around the house and upload chosen items with ease. The site uses a “claimed” tracking system, buyers can claim items they are interested in, leaving their name and number and choose a suitable selection time that the seller is available.

Sellers can track their items through various categories “Items listed, Available, Reserved and Collected.” Users will also have access to a direct messaging portal between the “buyer” and “seller”

How to use:

  1. “seller” uploads image and description of item they are looking to offload
  2. Interested “buyer” claims the item and selects a suitable time from the sellers offered times. Buyer can direct message the seller with queries they may have.
  3. Item is collected and Seller marks as claimed and collected as applicable.
