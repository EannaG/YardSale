Why this exists

Because a house is more than a list of things.

When a family is clearing a home, coordinating who wants what can become another job in an already busy time. YardSale helps keeping those decisions simple, respectful and organised.

Who is this for?

Family members organising the clearance of a relative's home following a move, downsizing or a bereavement.

What's the plan?

A free basic clearance with an optional paid upgrade for larger catalogues. This is an untested business-model assumption.

Current demo stores changes in one browser only. Live multi-device claiming and private organiser accounts would require a backend.

Behind the build

Made for the real mess of clearing a home.

YardSale is a mobile-friendly catalogue and collection organiser. Each item can be photographed, listed and reserved; organisers choose collection windows and track what has gone.

What works in this preview

Browse twelve sample listings, claim an item, upload photographs, set collection times, view item statuses, manage reservations and use the dedicated Messages tab to read enquiries and reply. Changes are saved in your current browser.

What a full launch would need

Secure accounts, a shared database to synchronise claims and messages across phones, notifications and stronger privacy protections. Nearby clearances are planned, not live.
